About Us

PicPitik is a small photography studio located in Nasugbu, Batangas. We provide photography services for different occasions such as birthdays, graduations, family events, and small businesses.

PicPitik started in 2024 with the goal of helping people save their important memories through photographs. We believe that every moment is worth remembering.
